About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Database Lab, School of Computing, National University of Singapore, with the supervision of Professor Tan Kian-Lee. I obtained my bachelor degree in Computer Science and Technology from Harbin Institute of Technology, China in 2014. I am currently interested in Dynamic Graph Management and Analytics on Parallel Processors, especially for GPU accelerators.

Latest Update: March, 2019
